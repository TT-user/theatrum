Service

Medical Nutrition Therapy (MNT)

MNT is an evidence-based, individualized nutrition treatment that uses your health history, labs and goals to build a plan that actually fits your life, not a generic diet sheet.

Conditions I work with

Diabetes (type 1, type 2 & gestational)
Cardiovascular disease
GI disorders (celiac, Crohn's, IBS, ulcerative colitis)
Kidney disease
Cancer
Weight management
Eating disorders
Food allergies & intolerances
Autoimmune conditions
HIV/AIDS & malnutrition
Pre/post-bariatric care
Sports nutrition

How sessions work

90 minutes

Initial Session

We go through your full health history, define your goals, and build a personalized care plan together. Most people choose to continue with weekly sessions after this.

60 minutes

Follow-Up Sessions

Collaborative check-ins on your goals, with worksheets and activities we can share on screen (virtual) or on paper (in-person).
