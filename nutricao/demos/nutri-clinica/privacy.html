Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Camila Prado ("we," "our," "the practice") handles the information of visitors to this website and of clients receiving nutrition counseling services.

1. Information we collect

This website does not include a direct intake form. Personal information reaches us only when you choose to share it, for example:

  • Your name and phone number when you reach out via WhatsApp to book a session;
  • Information you share directly by email or phone;
  • Basic technical browsing data (such as device type and approximate location) collected by third-party services embedded on this site, such as the Google Maps location widget.

2. Health information

Any health information shared once you become a client is handled separately from this website, through the scheduling and care channels (including telehealth) used by the practice. This website itself does not store or process clinical or health records.

3. How we use this information

Information shared to book a session or ask a question is used exclusively to:

  • Respond to inquiries and schedule sessions;
  • Provide continuity of care if you become a client.

4. Sharing with third parties

We do not sell or rent your personal information. Any scheduling or telehealth partners are governed by their own privacy policies, which we encourage you to review directly on their platforms.

5. Your rights

Depending on your state or country of residence, you may have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information. To exercise these rights, contact us using the information below.

6. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information shared through our contact and scheduling channels, but no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

7. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated periodically. The date of the last update is always shown at the top of this page.
